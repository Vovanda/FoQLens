# Visual metaphor

The quantization filter works like a multi-lens with zones of different focus: through it we look at the
network and take knowledge from it, and the lenses are chosen for the query. Through a frosted lens the
details are lost and the structure of things shows; through a clear one everything is seen in detail.

Behind the metaphor the mechanism allocates the precision of the weights by the meaning of the query
([precision-regulator.md](precision-regulator.md)). The metaphor itself can be played with on the main page
of the project's site.

The project's name, FoQLens, comes from it: to the author's ear a lens sounds softer than zones.
