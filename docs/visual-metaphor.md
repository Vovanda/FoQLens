# Visual metaphor

The metaphor of the mechanism, so that how it works is easier to grasp.

The quantization filter works like a multi-lens with zones of different focus: through it we look at the
network and take knowledge from it, and the lenses are chosen for the query. Through a frosted lens the
details are lost, but the structure of things shows; through a clear lens everything is seen in detail.

It is a metaphor, not the mechanism: the mechanism allocates the precision of the weights by the meaning of
the query ([quantization-filter.md](quantization-filter.md)). The metaphor can be seen, and played with, on the
main page of the project's site.

The name of the project, FoQLens, refers to this metaphor: to the author's ear it sounds softer and more
pleasant than zones.
