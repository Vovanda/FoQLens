Precision where the question is, not everywhere

A question about biology and a question about a proof do not need more or less precision. They need precision in different places.

Every quantization scheme decides precision per fixed unit - the model, a layer, a channel, a token, an expert. The boundaries come from the architecture; a controller only picks how many bits each unit gets.

FoQLens asks the model itself which weights matter for this query, reads them more precisely, and reads the rest coarsely. Mixture of Experts is the rigid special case of the same idea: experts with hard edges, fixed at training, opened by a router.

The mechanism is FoQZones - focusable quantization zones. Call it a Mixture of Zones if you like, though there is no router here and there will not be one. FoQLens is the project and the model that carries the regulator; the lens is its metaphor.

The metaphor above is drawn on real weights: the map of Gemma 4 E2B, 14 708 blocks of 64 output rows, blocks that light up together lying close. Place a zone on it and the regulator runs - the plate gives the cost in bits per weight.

Coarse pixels are weights read at few bits; sharp ones are read at many. Memory is not a budget you set - it is what your zones cost.

The goals

One set of weights that serves any device and any load, opening to full precision exactly where the query needs it.

On the device. One file of weights for a phone, glasses or a laptop: the precision follows the battery, the heat and the free memory, and the zones stay where the query is.

The price of a request. A simple question gets small zones and costs little; a hard one gets wider. The price of a token follows the question.

Agents and reasoning. A draft read mostly at base precision is refined step by step - the main hypothesis, tested when the model is there.

Graceful degradation. Under load or heat the model gets coarse first in what the query does not need.

What came out

Six experiments in - days of work, every number from a run that is in the repository. The maps here are built by oracles that see the right answer - a ceiling to aim at, not a mechanism.

What holds these numbers up: every prediction was committed to git before the run that tested it; the corpus is frozen - the questions, their wrappers and their reference answers do not move; every gain is measured against a control at memory equal to the byte; and every row below links the file the numbers were read from, raw answers included.

The mechanism

Three controls, each doing one thing: the base precision - what the whole network is read at before any zone is lifted, called floor in the runs and the scripts; focus area - the share of the network a zone covers; focus strength - how far their centers rise above it. The rules below are what the field above runs; the full text is in docs/precision-regulator.md.

The rules need a distance d between blocks and do not say which. In what space the zones of a query live, and what makes two weights close, is an open question of the problem statement; where zones grow from, along what, how far and how fast are strategies still to be tested (docs/zone-strategies.md).

ladder      ℓ₀ < ℓ₁ < … < ℓ_m          the levels this model can be read at; ℓ₀ = ZERO
base        G = ℓ_γ                     any rung; floor in the scripts
distance    d(a, b)                     between blocks; not chosen
width       D                           of the network in d

1 radius    R = f · D                   f = 0: the center only;   f = 1: the whole network
2 ceiling   κ = γ + ⌊g · (m − γ)⌋       g = 0: the zone is the base;   g = 1: the top rung
3 profile   stops s_κ < … < s_(γ+1)     where each ring ends, in radii; above 1, past the edge
4 lift      ρ_i(b) = d(b, c_i) / R,     L_i(b) = max(0, 1 − ρ_i(b) / s_last)   linear for now
5 combine   L(b) = min(1, Σ L_i)  or  max L_i
6 level     ρ* = s_last · (1 − L(b))    the ring that ρ* falls into; outside every zone: G
7 memory    bits = Σ w_b · bits(b) / Σ w_b

Nothing here names a level: the ladder comes from the model and its storage. On Gemma 4 E2B one stored copy - a k-quant base with refinements over it - is read at 2, 4, 6 or 8 bits, and the model holds no other weights - so the ladder is ZERO, D2, D4, D6, D8, and the regulator runs over all five.

The corpus

A layout can only be measured where the model answers from knowledge. So the questions are selected by measurement: a question stays if the full model answers it right in its own words. It never sees options, in any regime, and three judges read its answer - the SQuAD score, the full model itself grading it as an exam, and Claude. The corpus is frozen: 20,640 questions - 18,576 E2B-it knows and 2,064 it does not.

RegimeWhere the answer isWhat the zones should do
Readingin the passage - SQuAD v2little: the knowledge came in with the prompt
Knowledgeonly in the weights - TriviaQA, NQ-open, ARC-Challenge and ARC-Easy without their optionsdecide - the regime the idea is about
Two stepssplit across two passages - HotpotQAmatter, and less than in the second

If the zones help as much where the answer sits in the context as where it is only in the weights, the mechanism is not doing what it claims - and that is the test the old corpus could not run. How a corpus is chosen: docs/corpus.md.
